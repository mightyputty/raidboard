Keep if you see it

Found-in-raid turn-ins for your quests. These are not tied to a map — pick them up in any raid and they stop being a chore later.

How the score works

Every quest you can currently accept is broken into its raid objectives. A map earns 3 points for each quest it would finish outright, 1 point for each objective that can only be done there, and 0.4 for each objective you could also do somewhere else. Optional objectives count half. Turn-ins, trader levels and skill grinds score nothing — they are not raid work.

Where to go

Objectives

Mark Active for anything sitting in your in-game quest list — that is what the raid board scores. Marking a quest Done also marks everything it required, so you can set up a whole chain from its last quest.

Story chapters

The ten chapters that lead to an ending. They have no trader, tarkov.dev does not carry them, and nothing in the game logs reports their progress — so these are yours to tick. Objectives that name a map count towards the Raid Board score and show up on that map's plan; talking to a trader and handing things in score nothing, same as anywhere else.

What this does to your machine

Your logs are opened read-only. Nothing is ever written into the Escape from Tarkov folder, no game memory is read, and nothing is injected into the game. This works the same way TarkovMonitor does — by reading log files the game already writes for itself.

Your progress stays in this browser. There is no account and no server to send it to. Use Export below if you want a copy.

One thing does leave your machine: opening a Guide loads its screenshots from the Escape from Tarkov Wiki, so the wiki sees that request. Nothing about your account, your progress or your logs goes with it.
